Response Time

We typically respond within 24–48 hours. To help us assist you faster, please include:

  • Your device type (iPhone, Android, tablet)
  • A description of the issue or question
  • Any screenshots that might help illustrate the problem

In-App Support

You can also contact us directly from within the app by going to Settings → Contact Us. This automatically includes your device and app version info, which helps us troubleshoot faster.
