Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: April 25, 2026

At Daisy's Dailies, you have full control over your data. This page explains how to delete your data from our app.

1. Delete Specific Data (Keep Your Account)

You can delete individual pieces of data without deleting your entire account:

Delete Task Data (Flowers, Petals, Items)

To delete a flower (and all its petals and items):

  1. Open the Daisy's Dailies app
  2. Long-press on the flower you want to delete
  3. Select "Edit Flower" or tap the settings icon
  4. Scroll down and tap "Delete Flower"
  5. Confirm the deletion

To delete a petal (single stop/location):

  1. Open the flower containing the petal
  2. Tap on the petal you want to delete
  3. In the petal detail modal, tap the settings icon
  4. Select "Delete Petal"
  5. Confirm the deletion

To delete individual items from a petal:

  1. Open the petal containing the item
  2. Swipe left on the item you want to delete
  3. Tap the delete icon

Delete Location Data

  1. Tap on any petal that has a location
  2. In the petal detail modal, tap on the location field
  3. Clear the location or select "Remove Location"
  4. Save your changes

Leave a Family

If you're not the family owner:

  1. Go to Settings → Family
  2. Tap "Leave Family"
  3. Confirm your decision

This removes your access to the family's shared data and removes you from the family member list.

Note: If you're the family owner, you must either transfer ownership or delete the family before leaving.

2. Delete Your Account (All Data)

Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data from Daisy's Dailies.

What Gets Deleted:

  • Your user account and profile information (name, email, avatar)
  • All your preferences and settings
  • Your subscription/purchase history
  • All data you created (flowers, petals, items, locations)
  • Your family membership
  • If you're a family owner: the entire family and all its data

Steps to Delete Your Account:

  1. Open the Daisy's Dailies app
  2. Go to the Settings tab (bottom navigation)
  3. Scroll down to the "Account" section
  4. Tap "Delete Account"
  5. Read the warning carefully
  6. If you signed up with email/password: enter your password to confirm
  7. If you signed up with Google: re-authenticate with Google to confirm
  8. Tap "Confirm Delete"
⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. If you're a family owner, all family members will lose access to the shared family data.

3. Data Deletion Timeline

  • Immediate: Data is removed from the app and becomes inaccessible to you and other users
  • Within 30 days: Data is permanently deleted from all our servers and backups
  • Third-party services: Data shared with third parties (Google Maps, RevenueCat) follows their respective deletion policies

4. What Happens to Family Data?

If you're a family member (not the owner):

  • Your personal account data is deleted
  • You're removed from the family member list
  • Data you created (flowers, petals, items) remains with the family
  • Your name is removed from items you added or completed

If you're the family owner:

  • The entire family is deleted
  • All family members lose access to the shared data
  • All flowers, petals, and items in the family are permanently deleted
  • All family members are notified that the family has been deleted

5. Automatic Data Cleanup

Certain data types are automatically deleted to keep your account organized:

Appointments: Appointments are automatically deleted 30 days after their scheduled date. This cleanup runs daily and cannot be undone. If you need to keep a record of past appointments, we recommend noting them elsewhere before the 30-day window expires.

6. Data Retained After Deletion

For legal and security purposes, we may retain certain data for a limited time:

  • Support tickets: Kept for up to 2 years for customer service purposes
  • Transaction records: Retained as required by law (typically 7 years) for tax and accounting purposes
  • Fraud prevention logs: May be retained to prevent abuse

This retained data is not accessible through the app and is stored securely in compliance with applicable laws.

7. Need Help?

If you're having trouble deleting your data or have questions about the deletion process, please contact us:

  • Email: daisysdailies@sibertstrategygroup.com
  • In-app: Settings → Contact Us

We'll respond within 48 hours and help you delete your data or answer any questions.

8. Request Data Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app or prefer to request deletion via email:

  1. Send an email to daisysdailies@sibertstrategygroup.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request"
  3. Include the email address associated with your account
  4. Specify what you want deleted (entire account or specific data)
  5. We'll verify your identity and process your request within 30 days

9. Third-Party Services

Daisy's Dailies uses the following third-party services that may retain data according to their own policies:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and database services - Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management - RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps Platform: Location services - Google Privacy Policy

When you delete your Daisy's Dailies account, we delete your data from our systems, but these third parties maintain their own data retention policies.

For more information about how we collect, use, and protect your data, please see our Privacy Policy.
